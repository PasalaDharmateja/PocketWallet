## Full Description (4000 chars max)
PocketWallet is a smart personal finance app that helps you organise your money into dedicated "pockets" — so you always know what's available for rent, groceries, entertainment, and savings.

🏦 POCKET BUDGETING
Create up to 100 named pockets, each with a priority level. Move money between your main wallet and pockets instantly. See exactly where every rupee sits.

💸 UPI PAYMENTS
Send money via UPI ID, phone number, or QR code scan. Supports GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM with one tap. No extra apps needed.

📅 SCHEDULED PAYMENTS
Set up automatic payments — one-time or recurring (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly). PocketWallet runs them automatically at the right time. Pause, resume, or edit anytime.

📊 MONTHLY REPORTS
Visual spending breakdown by pocket. See where your money went each month with clear charts and category totals.

🔐 BANK-LEVEL SECURITY
• 4-digit PIN with biometric unlock (Face ID / Fingerprint)
• All data stored locally on your device
• No third-party data sharing

🔔 SMART NOTIFICATIONS
Get alerts for scheduled payments, low balances, and successful transfers.
